## Embedded obligations / review limits

The accepted 27 A families, three H families and C reference family are all represented. Permanent obligations inside historical events additionally remain represented in candidate architecture, evidence, lifecycle and protection contracts: sole drivetrain/localization owner, O/E distinction, guarded resets/vision, immutable input validity, safe-stop attempts, F01 execution barrier/fatal precedence and F03 normal scheduler refresh. Protected rules preserve the sensitive-file prohibition and prior incident without reading protected contents. Evidence retains CF-U/PF-U/Phase-2 qualifications, H1 provisional semantics, physical deferrals and source/current/history distinctions.

Root-inline identity, roles, scope, ownership and stop/HOLD expectations are deliberately duplicated into detailed rules to protect partial loading. M00 bilingual and five-label student evidence obligations keep module-specific applicability; extra software/build/Glass/DS evidence descriptions do not silently replace that vocabulary. External human-input exception remains narrow.

All source bytes remain recoverable, but byte identity does not prove authority equivalence, one-concept meaning, bilingual fidelity, scheduler/localization semantics or evidence sufficiency. These remain human/Architect review obligations. G4 population self-check is not independent G5 verification, G6 approval or G7 activation.
